Obscurify is a private, anonymous AI chat app with more than 300 AI models in one place. Ask questions, write, translate, read documents, search the web and run code, and switch models whenever you like. You don't need an email, phone number or password. Chat as a guest straight away, or make an account code on your phone.

ALL THE MAJOR AI MODELS IN ONE APP
Chat with models from OpenAI (GPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), DeepSeek, Qwen, Meta (Llama), Mistral, xAI (Grok), Moonshot (Kimi), Z.ai (GLM) and many open models. Search the full list, browse by category (Frontier, Reasoning, Cheap, Writing, Code tools, Translation, Documents, Zero retention and more), keep favorites, and change models in the middle of a conversation. Start with a free model, even as a guest.

ANONYMOUS BY DESIGN
• No email, phone number or password. Your account is a 20-character account code made on your phone.
• Guests can chat without making an account at all.
• Your chats are stored on your phone. Sync between your devices and the website is end-to-end encrypted with a key from your account code, so the server keeps only ciphertext.
• Temp chats are never saved on your phone, and Wipe deletes every chat, on the phone and in sync.
• Choose zero-retention models, served by providers whose policy is not to keep your prompts.
• No ads.

ENCRYPTED AI CHAT
Encrypted mode talks to models running inside confidential-computing enclaves (TEE). Before anything is sent, the phone checks the enclave's attestation and encrypts your message to it, so Obscurify's own server passes it along without being able to read it. Each answer shows whether every check passed.

AN AI ASSISTANT THAT DOES THINGS
• Web search
• Attach photos, PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV and text or code files. Documents are read on your phone and sent as text, so every model can use them.
• Code: the assistant runs Python and JavaScript on your phone to calculate, analyze data and draw charts, and builds small pages you can open full screen
• Leave the app while an answer is written and you get a notification when it's ready. Signed in, an answer the phone couldn't finish is finished for you and waits in the chat.
• Streamed answers, collapsible reasoning, copy, edit, retry and stop

A FAST, SMALL APP
Obscurify is a small native app with a minimalist black design.

SAFETY AND CONTROL
Report any answer from inside the app. Delete your account from the app at any time.
