Obscurify is a private, anonymous AI chat app with over 300 AI models. No email, phone number or password required.

Ask a question, search the web, run code, write, translate, or have it read a document. Switch models whenever you like, even mid-conversation. Start chatting for free as a guest right away, and create an account (an account code your phone generates) when you're ready.

THE MODELS
GPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama (Meta), Mistral, Grok (xAI), Kimi (Moonshot AI), GLM (Z.ai) and many other open models.
Search the list of 300+ models or browse it by category (Frontier, Reasoning, Cheap, Writing, Code tools, Translation, Documents, Zero retention, etc). Keep a list of your favorite models. You can start with a free model, even as a guest.

YOUR ACCOUNT AND YOUR DATA
• An account is a 20-character code, generated by your phone. Guests don't need one.
• Chats are stored on your phone. If you sync your chats with your other devices or the website, they're end-to-end encrypted with a key made from your account code, so only ciphertext is stored on the server.
• A temporary chat isn't stored on your phone. You can wipe your chats (on your phone and in sync) at any time.
• A model labelled "zero retention" is served by a provider whose policy is not to store your prompts.
• No ads.

ENCRYPTED MODE
In Encrypted mode your message is routed to an AI model in a secure "confidential computing" environment (a trusted execution environment, or TEE). Your phone first validates the TEE attestation, then encrypts your message to the TEE. The message is relayed by the Obscurify server but can only be read in the TEE. Each response shows whether all checks passed.

TOOLS
• Web search
• Attach photos and documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text or code) to your messages. Documents are read on your phone and sent to the model as text, so every model can use them.
• Run code. The assistant runs Python and JavaScript programs on your phone. Crunch numbers, analyze data, make charts. It can even build web pages for you to view full screen.
• Leave the app while an answer is being generated and get a notification when it's done. When you're signed in, an answer your phone couldn't finish is finished for you and waiting in the chat.
• See answers stream in as they're generated. The models' reasoning is collapsible. Stop, copy, edit or retry any answer.
