Obscurify is private AI chat with hundreds of models behind one anonymous account. There's no email, username or password, only a 16-digit account number.

• Chat with leading and open models, with streamed answers, web search and image attachments
• Your chats are stored only on your phone, never on our servers
• Search models, browse them by category and keep favorites
• Free models to start
• Report any answer from inside the app
• Delete your account from the app at any time

The app is a small, fast native client. You must be 18 or older to use Obscurify.
